# Safeguard policy

Genesis 2–4 is quoted to justify who leads, who is to blame, who must forgive and who may
rule. In abusive relationships these lines are used as weapons. The app has to make that
harder, not easier.

## Rules for content

1. **Describe, never prescribe.** Cards on 3:16, *ezer*, "headship" or forgiveness say what the
   text does and does not say. They never tell a reader what a spouse should do.
2. **Show both sides of a contested reading** (complementarian/egalitarian; Adam present/absent)
   and say who holds each. Never rank blame between the sexes.
3. **Mercy is not an obligation.** Any card on forgiveness or mercy (Cain, 4:15) says that
   God's mercy is not a rule that a victim must forgive or return.
4. **Care note.** A card gets a `safeguard` field if it touches gender roles, blame, authority,
   forgiveness or violence. The note appears on the card with a link to the Help page.
   Current cards: zakar-remember, cain-repents, eve-adds-touch, ezer-helper,
   failure-to-lead, lamech-boast, god-cursed-eve, mark-of-cain (racist misuse);
   insight: desire-and-rule-echo.
5. **Group plans** carry a leader note on evenings 2–4: no personal stories about a partner,
   no assigning blame between the sexes, say aloud that forgiveness is not owed.
6. **No tracking.** No analytics, accounts or cloud storage. Progress stays in the browser
   (`localStorage`) on the device. A shared device is a risk, so the Help page contains no
   history and the quiz stores only a best score.
7. **Quick exit.** To do before release: add a "leave quickly" button on the Help page that
   replaces the page with a neutral site.
