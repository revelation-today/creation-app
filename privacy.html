In short

Eden Check has no accounts, no statistics, no advertising and no tracking. It sets no cookies and builds no profiles. Your progress stays on your device. The app collects no data.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The web version of the app is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the web version, GitHub processes the technical data it needs to deliver the files and secure its service, in particular your IP address, the date and time, the address requested and details of your browser or device. The iPhone app stores its content in the app itself and does not contact GitHub for it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is delivering the app securely and reliably. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Data on your device

Your reading-plan progress, your quiz best score and your settings (language, text size, appearance) are stored on your device. The web version also keeps a copy of itself for offline reading. This data does not leave your device. Storing it is strictly necessary for features you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You delete it by clearing the website's data in your browser or by removing the app.

Links

The Help page links to support organisations. Data goes to those sites only when you tap a link; the site's own privacy policy then applies.

Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is answering your enquiry. We delete messages once they have been dealt with and no retention duty applies.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Write to the email address above. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), the competent one being the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
