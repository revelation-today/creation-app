# Result in one line

The critique in `paradise.md` holds up on the main points, but **several of its verdicts were
too strong or are unverified**. Fix these before they go into cards or the book.

# Corrections to paradise.md

| Item | paradise.md said | Research found | New verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cain repents (4:13) | "Wrong fact" | *avon* means guilt **or** punishment; ESV footnote "my guilt is too great to bear". Most commentators (Henry, Gill) read an impenitent complaint. No confession either way. | 🟠 contested; claim "no repentance recorded" is safe |
| Cain = "acquired one" | Standard etymology | Pun with *qanah* is deliberate, but many link *qayin* to "smith/spear" (Tubal-Cain, Kenites). "Treasure" is still unsupported. "With the help of" is a translators' addition. | 🟠 |
| *zakar* "distinct roots" | Stated as fact | BDB calls the relation "obscure"; Proto-Semitic reconstructions list noun and verb apart; some popular sources derive noun from verb. HALOT not read. | Gen 3:15 "Jewish exegesis traditionally non-messianic" | Stated flatly | Targums are mixed (collective/moral reading, but Yerushalmi mentions "days of King Messiah"). # Confirmed

- Day 5 birds, Day 6 land animals and humans (text itself).
- *ish/ishah* is the real pun; ish and ishah are probably not cognate (NET note).
- Genesis 1 and 2 differ in divine name and order; explanations compete (P/J vs. complementary).
- Firstlings and fat only for Abel; Hebrews 11:4 gives "faith".
- *ezer* is used of God; Freedman (BAR 9:1) and Meyers argue strength/equality; critics push back.

# Not verified (still primary-todo)

Barr (1961); HALOT on *zākār*, *ʿēzer*, *qayin*; Targum texts on 3:15; Genesis Rabbah 19:3 and
ARN A1 on 3:3; Wenham/Walton/Sarna/Westermann commentaries; Day (1985) and Levenson (1988) on
chaos; Memphite theology.

scholars on both sides (Freedman and Meyers vs. their critics; Day vs. Zhakevich; Levenson vs.
Tsumura). That is safer than an unbacked claim, and it is a good result, not a loss.
