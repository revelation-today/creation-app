Eden Check: support

Eden Check — what Genesis 1–4 really says. By Hagen Schilder.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or a correction to a card: revelation-today@web.de. Please name the card or page and what you saw. If you think a card is wrong, say which claim and which source you rely on; every card lists its sources and how far they have been checked, and corrections are welcome.

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection?
No. All content is bundled in the app and works offline.

Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your device: reading-plan progress and your quiz best score. Deleting the app removes it.

Will it tell me what to do in my marriage or family?
No. The app describes what the text says and does not say. It does not give personal advice, it shows both sides of contested readings, and it does not rank blame between men and women.

If these texts have been used against you

Genesis 2–4 is sometimes quoted to justify control, blame or silence in relationships. That use is not what the text says, and the app says so on the cards concerned. If you are being hurt or controlled, or are afraid, please talk to someone you trust or a local support service. The app's Help page lists support lines. In an emergency call your local emergency number.
